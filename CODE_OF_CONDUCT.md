# Community conduct

Elixpo expects participation to remain respectful, specific, and focused on the
work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misattribution,
and sustained disruption are not accepted.

When discussing research, critique methods, evidence, and reproducibility—not
the dignity or identity of the people involved. Disagreement is welcome when it
is supported, proportionate, and open to correction.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route published at
[elixpo.com/community](https://elixpo.com/community). Maintainers may edit,
hide, lock, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary
to protect contributors and the integrity of the public record.
