# Support

Use [Elixpo Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/elixpo/discussions) for public
questions about the research directory or publication format. Use this
repository's issue tracker for reproducible site defects.

For a factual correction, cite the affected record and primary evidence. For
an authorship, license, review-status, or research conclusion dispute, contact
the owning research repository first; this site does not adjudicate scientific
claims or replace its maintainers.

Elixpo Research is community-maintained and does not promise a response time,
publication acceptance, peer review, hosting, compute, or permanent artifact
availability.
