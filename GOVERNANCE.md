# Governance

Elixpo Research is maintained as a core Elixpo publication surface.

The canonical registry in `elixpo/elixpo` owns organization classification,
research status, editorial descriptions, and cross-project relationships. This
repository owns the research-specific interface, feeds, citation exports,
crawl metadata, accessibility, and deployment configuration. Owning research
repositories retain authority over their source, evidence, releases, and
artifact licenses.

The lead maintainer may merge presentation and maintenance changes after the
required automated checks pass. Changes to authorship, review claims, licenses,
or research conclusions must be made at their authoritative source and then
synchronized here. Elixpo does not promise peer review or certification.
